# SuperFinz — Landing page override

Overrides Master for `/` only. The product app keeps Master exactly.

## Direction

Editorial Swiss fintech. Alternating deep-navy and paper bands so the page is never a dark wall. One idea carries the page: uneven income becomes a steady paycheck. That idea is drawn once as a scroll-driven chart and echoed in the copy, not restated in cards.

## Type

- Display: Gatwick Ultrabold 800 for headlines, tracking −0.04em, line-height 0.95. Gatwick Ultralight 200 for oversized figures.
- Body: Geist Sans 400/500, 17–19px, 65ch max.
- Data: Geist Mono for currency, dates, ticker, labels. `tabular-nums` everywhere digits align.

## Rules

- No gradients, glass, purple, neon, or emoji.
- Numbered markers only for the four-step flow (it is a sequence).
- Motion: the scroll morph and a settlements ticker. Reduced motion renders both final states statically.
